# Enterprise AI Framework v0.2.0

**Release date:** 2026-07-28  
**Status:** Draft  
**License:** CC BY 4.0

## Executive summary

This release adds a **Delivery** section — an operating model for AI-assisted software
delivery — and consolidates the Enterprise Context Architecture (ECA) remodel. It is aimed
at architects, governance leads, and delivery teams in government, public-sector, and
regulated organizations adopting AI-assisted development while preserving accountability.

## Highlights

- New **Delivery** section: specification-driven delivery, roles and accountability, and
  team topologies for AI-assisted delivery.
- A RACI mapping delivery roles to the governance lifecycle gates, reconciled with the
  named accountability owners in the governance operating model.
- Four hero infographics with descriptive alt text, plus a Delivery navigation section and
  home-page card.
- Enterprise Context Architecture (ECA) established as the flagship model with a dedicated
  ECA × TOGAF ADM page and reference diagrams.

## Detailed changes

### Added

- Delivery section pages: overview, specification-driven delivery, roles and accountability,
  and team topologies.
- Delivery hero infographics, navigation section, home-page card, and a governance
  cross-link to the delivery model.
- ECA × TOGAF ADM page, context-wheel and ADM-cycle diagrams, and context ownership and
  right-sizing guidance.

### Changed

- Adopted Enterprise Context Architecture (ECA) as the flagship model, with seven context
  domains centred on Requirements Management as a cross-cutting perspective on the TOGAF ADM.
- Folded financial considerations into the Business, Operational, and Technology contexts.

### Deprecated or removed

- None.

### Security and safety

- No security-specific changes in this release.

## Architecture impact

Introduces a delivery operating model that expresses the governance lifecycle gates and
accountability owners at the team level. No changes to trust boundaries, integration
services, or control expectations.

## Assurance and compliance impact

No changes to compliance mappings or control catalogs. The delivery model reinforces
existing accountability and evidence expectations; it does not imply certification.

## Compatibility and migration

Pre-1.0 draft; content and structure may still change incompatibly. The delivery FTE figures
are illustrative examples, not prescribed team sizes.

## Known limitations

The delivery model covers specification-driven delivery, roles, and team topologies; QA and
review at AI scale, governance quality gates, shadow-application controls, and scaling across
many teams are deferred to later releases.
