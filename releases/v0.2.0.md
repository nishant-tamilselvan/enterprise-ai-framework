# Enterprise AI Framework v0.2.0

**Release date:** 2026-07-28  
**Status:** Draft  
**License:** CC BY 4.0

## Executive summary

This release adds a **Delivery** operating model for AI-assisted software delivery and
the final Enterprise Context Architecture (ECA) overview diagram. It serves architects,
governance leads, and delivery teams in government, public-sector, and regulated
organizations that use AI-assisted development while preserving accountability.

## Highlights

- New **Delivery** section: specification-driven delivery, roles and accountability, and
  team topologies for AI-assisted delivery.
- A RACI mapping delivery roles to the governance lifecycle gates, reconciled with the
  named accountability owners in the governance operating model.
- Four hero infographics with descriptive alt text, plus a Delivery navigation section and
  home-page card.
- The final ECA × TOGAF ADM overview diagram with a figure credit, plus a landing page,
  logo, and social cards. ECA itself shipped in 0.1.0.

## Detailed changes

### Added

- Delivery section pages: overview, specification-driven delivery, roles and accountability,
  and team topologies.
- Delivery hero infographics, navigation section, home-page card, and a governance
  cross-link to the delivery model.
- Final ECA × TOGAF ADM overview diagram, replacing the draft diagram.
- Landing page, custom logo, social cards, and navigation polish.

### Changed

- Page frontmatter drops the `status` field, which MkDocs Material reserves for navigation
  badges.

### Deprecated or removed

- None.

### Security and safety

- This release leaves security guidance unchanged.

## Architecture impact

The release introduces a delivery operating model that expresses governance lifecycle gates
and accountability owners at the team level. Trust boundaries, integration services, and
control expectations remain unchanged.

## Assurance and compliance impact

Compliance mappings and control catalogs remain unchanged. The delivery model reinforces
existing accountability and evidence expectations. Certification remains outside its
scope.

## Compatibility and migration

This pre-1.0 draft may introduce incompatible content and structure changes. The delivery
FTE figures illustrate one possible allocation. Teams should tailor their allocation to
mission, risk, and scale.

## Known limitations

The delivery model covers specification-driven delivery, roles, and team topologies. Later
releases will address quality assurance and review at AI scale, governance quality gates,
shadow-application controls, and coordination across many teams.
