# Compliance mappings are informative

> Draft from the Enterprise AI Framework. Review it through your own governance, then set `status: Approved` before you rely on it.

A mapping from architecture capabilities to an external framework, such as the EU AI Act or ISO/IEC 42001, supports traceability only.

1. A mapping never claims legal advice, audit, certification, authorization, or conformity assessment.
2. Each mapping records the source, its edition, and the date someone last checked its facts.
3. A compliance claim needs evidence that controls are implemented, operate effectively, and suit the specific system.
4. Qualified legal, compliance, security, privacy, and audit reviewers confirm applicability before anyone relies on a mapping.

Source: [Compliance mappings](https://nishant-tamilselvan.github.io/enterprise-ai-framework/compliance/overview/)
