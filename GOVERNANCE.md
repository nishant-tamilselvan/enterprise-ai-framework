# Project Governance

## Purpose

This document defines transparent stewardship for the Enterprise AI Framework.

## Roles

### Maintainers

Maintainers approve releases, protect project integrity, administer infrastructure, and resolve routine contributions. The initial maintainer is NISHANT TAMILSELVAN.

### Editors

Editors coordinate one or more architecture domains, ensure source quality, and maintain terminology and document status.

### Contributors and reviewers

Contributors propose changes. Reviewers provide domain, implementation, security, accessibility, policy, or editorial expertise.

## Decision model

The project seeks evidence-based consensus. Routine editorial corrections may be merged by one maintainer. Substantive architecture, normative, governance, or licensing changes require public discussion and approval from at least two maintainers when two are available.

If consensus cannot be reached, maintainers record options, evidence, objections, and the final decision in the relevant issue. The lead maintainer is the final decision-maker until a multi-maintainer steering group is established.

## Transparency and conflicts

Material decisions occur in public issues and pull requests except embargoed security matters or legally protected information. Participants disclose conflicts that could reasonably affect impartiality. Vendors must not use project participation to imply product endorsement.

## Releases

- Patch releases contain corrections and non-substantive clarification.
- Minor releases add compatible capabilities, mappings, or blueprints.
- Major releases may change foundational models, terminology, or normative expectations.

Stable releases require a changelog, release notes, successful automated checks, and a documented review window.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes require a public proposal, at least 14 calendar days for comment, and maintainer approval. The project may establish a diverse steering committee as participation grows.
