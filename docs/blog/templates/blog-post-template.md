# Post title

Copy this file to `docs/blog/posts/YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md`, then replace this instructional front matter with:

- `date: YYYY-MM-DD`
- `categories:` followed by one or more categories
- optional `draft: true` until publication

## Summary

State the update and why it matters in two or three sentences.

## Context

Explain the problem, affected audience, evidence, and relevant framework version.

## What changed

Describe architecture, governance, security, compliance, or editorial changes. Link to changed documents and decisions.

## Impact and migration

Identify compatibility, implementation, risk, or governance implications and required actions.

## Evidence and references

Cite authoritative sources and distinguish verified facts, interpretation, and opinion.

## Get involved

Link issues, discussions, public-review dates, and contribution guidance.

> Disclosure: List author affiliations, sponsorships, product interests, or state “No material conflicts disclosed.”
