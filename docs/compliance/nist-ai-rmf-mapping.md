# NIST AI RMF mapping

This initial mapping uses the four NIST AI RMF 1.0 functions. Future revisions will map categories and subcategories in detail and account for applicable NIST profiles and playbook guidance.

| Function | Framework alignment | Example evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GOVERN | Governance operating model, policy framework, inventory, accountability, risk acceptance | Charters, policies, RACI, inventory, approvals, training records |
| MAP | Context domains, intended/prohibited use, affected parties, system boundary, impact assessment | Context record, impact assessment, architecture diagrams, data lineage |
| MEASURE | Evaluation service, risk tiers, performance and harm metrics, independent assurance | Evaluation plan/results, red-team report, control tests, uncertainty analysis |
| MANAGE | Lifecycle gates, risk treatment, deployment constraints, monitoring, incidents, retirement | Risk register, authorization, SLOs, incident records, change decisions |

## Tailoring questions

- Which NIST AI RMF profile applies to the organizational and use-case context?
- How will the organization translate trustworthiness characteristics into measurable requirements?
- Who evaluates the system independently of model development and service ownership?
- How do operational events feed back into GOVERN, MAP, and MEASURE activities?

Source: National Institute of Standards and Technology, *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)*, NIST AI 100-1 (2023). Confirm current NIST guidance before use.
